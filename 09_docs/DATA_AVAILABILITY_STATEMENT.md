# Manuscript-ready Data Availability statement

## Recommended English version

The reproducibility package for this study is publicly archived at **[ZENODO DOI]** and mirrored at **https://github.com/joyj06554-dev/ec-ev-repro-pack**. It contains the complete Web of Science Core Collection search strategy and search history, stepwise record counts, the documented database-refinement workflow, a record-level public index for the final 4,974 records, the raw author-keyword dictionary, validation scripts, analysis metadata, software-version information, supporting search evidence, and SHA-256 integrity manifests. Bulk Web of Science Full Record and Cited References exports are not redistributed publicly because access and redistribution are subject to the applicable database licence. The original 5,367-record pre-refinement export was not retained; therefore, record-level provenance for the 393 records removed by the combined document-type, publication-year, and language refinement is unavailable. The complete query, refinement criteria, search date, and aggregate counts are archived to permit methodological reproduction. Because bibliographic databases are updated over time, a later re-run may yield counts that differ from the historical search.

## 中文核对稿

本研究的可再现性资料包公开存档于 **[ZENODO DOI]**，并同步托管于 **https://github.com/joyj06554-dev/ec-ev-repro-pack**。资料包包括完整的 Web of Science Core Collection 检索策略及检索历史、各步骤记录数、数据库联合限定流程、最终 4,974 条记录的逐条公开索引、原始作者关键词词典、数据核验脚本、分析参数与软件版本信息、检索证据文件及 SHA-256 完整性清单。受数据库许可约束，Web of Science 的 Full Record and Cited References 批量原始导出文件不在公共仓库中再分发。由于最初 5,367 条记录的限定前导出文件未被保留，故无法提供经文献类型、发表年份和语言联合限定所排除 393 条记录的逐条来源信息；但完整检索式、限定条件、检索日期和各步骤总数均已归档，以支持方法学复现。鉴于文献数据库会持续更新，未来重新执行相同检索时所得记录数可能与历史检索存在差异。
