**Description**  
Reproducibility materials for a bibliometric and altmetric study of endothelial-cell and extracellular-vesicle literature. The archive contains the complete Web of Science Core Collection search strategy and historical search counts, database-refinement provenance, a minimized record-level index for the final 4,974 records, raw author-keyword frequencies, validation/regeneration scripts, analysis metadata, software versions, supporting evidence and SHA-256 integrity manifests. Bulk WoS Full Record and Cited References exports are not redistributed. The pre-refinement 5,367-record export was not retained; therefore, record-level provenance for the 393 records removed by the combined database refinement is unavailable.
